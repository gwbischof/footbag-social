# Feed polish bar

The feed should feel as finished as Instagram's. Not its features or scale — its
*feel*. Each line is checkable on a phone-width screen; "done" means seen working,
not compiled.

## Speed you can feel
1. **Nothing jumps.** Media boxes are sized before a byte arrives; images fade in
   over a blurhash; skeletons match the real card.
2. **Leaving and coming back costs nothing.** Switching tabs and returning to the
   feed shows the same posts at the same scroll position, instantly — no reload,
   no spinner.
3. **Every tap answers at once.** A ♦ lights before the server replies; a posted
   comment appears immediately; a new post is at the top when the composer closes.
4. **Smooth scrolling.** Images are decoded at the size they are drawn, and only
   the clip on screen plays.

## Gestures people already know
5. Double-tap gives a ♦, with a burst and (on phones) a haptic tap.
6. Swipe through a carousel; dots and a counter follow. Arrows with a mouse.
7. Clips autoplay muted when on screen, pause off it; one tap toggles sound.
8. Pull to refresh. Tapping the Feed tab while on the feed scrolls to the top.
9. **Comments open over the feed** as a sheet — tap the bubble and you are typing;
   swipe it away and you are where you were.

## Quiet chrome
10. The header is one row. Content starts within ~120px of the top.
11. Captions truncate with an inline "… more", never a dangling line.
12. One loud action per screen. Everything else is outline or text.

## The things this app is for, each obvious
13. Post a clip or carousel, tag the trick, mark it a tutorial — from the feed
    header, in under 30 seconds.
14. Find tutorials for a trick: tap a trick tag → its page → Tutorials.
15. Find what is near you: one chip.
16. Report a post, delete or edit your own: from the ⋯ menu, two taps.
17. See who posted: tap the name → profile grid.

## Not in scope
Stories, saves, shares to other apps, follows, comment likes, mentions, filters
on photos. Each adds machinery for something this community has not asked for.
