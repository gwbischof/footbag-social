A goal is a favourited trick or string — there is no separate goal with a target and a counter. That keeps one list instead of two, and nothing can go stale: you stop working on something by unfavouriting it.
This is where Try next stops being decoration and becomes the thing you actually do. It also records intent, not just outcome — knowing what someone is working on and failing at is better signal than knowing only what they landed.
